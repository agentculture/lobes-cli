# Delivery Summary — lane safety and quality (#304 #307 #308 #309)

plan: `lane-safety-and-quality-304-307-308-309` · run: `partial` · date: `2026-10-17`
baseline: `devague summary skeleton`

## Intent

> lobes lanes boot, compile and run safely: GPU services hold after a power cycle, the vLLM compile cache survives a recreate, a hot box sheds load instead of power-cycling, and clocks stay stock outside an explicit benchmark window

After: After a power cycle every lobes service waits until host uptime >= 120 s; a recreate reuses the lane's compile cache; a repeatedly crashing lane backs off instead of looping into held memory; a box over its per-card thermal threshold sheds sheddable tiers with 429 instead of heating further; lobes benchmark runs inside a locked-clocks window delegated to the machine CLI and always restores stock DVFS, recording conditions in the result

The run executed the 20-task plan `docs/plans/2026-10-17-lane-safety-and-quality-304-307-308-309.md`
in seven waves through `/assign-to-workforce`. The **Thor was code-only** throughout
(frame boundary c2): its experimental work was never deployed to, restarted or recreated.
