## Open now: the eugr GB10 nightly test (d4)

A strict tool call **with thinking** on the cortex lane repeats the tool call
about 78 times until `max_tokens`. That happens on **both** official `v0.30.0`
and `v0.31.0` (3 of 3 runs each). The nightly makes one call in 51 tokens.

What has been ruled out:

- **The grammar.** Identical across all three engines.
- **xgrammar's enforcement.** An offline matcher test showed it identical too:
  after one call, both EOS and another `<tool_call>` are allowed.
- **xgrammar's version.** Lapse l10: an early guess blamed xgrammar
  0.2.3 → 0.2.7, and that is wrong.

The cause is elsewhere in vLLM between 0.26.1rc1 and 0.30.0. DSpark speculation
is untested as a factor. Strict **without** thinking is clean, the Thor worker
(plain `qwen3_coder`) is clean, and `GATEWAY_FORCE_STRICT_TOOLS` is unset on
all boxes.

**In flight:**

```bash

```

This is vLLM 0.32.0rc1.dev301, `TORCH_CUDA_ARCH_LIST=12.1a`, the engine
sparkrun's official Qwen3.8-27B recipe uses. A background watcher prints the

## Deviations (all approved)

- **d1 (Thor):** on 0.31.0 / sm_110 the worker's ViT misread images until
  `--mm-encoder-attn-backend=TORCH_SDPA` was set in an override `command:`.
  R2 needs a template knob for it.
- **d2 (Spark):** `docker update --cpus` revokes **new** GPU access under the
  systemd cgroup driver (`NVMLError_Unknown`). Fixed by explicit `devices:`
  (`/dev/nvidia0`, `nvidiactl`, `nvidia-modeset`, `nvidia-uvm`,
  `nvidia-uvm-tools`) on `vllm-primary`. Governor bands raised to 92/95/98 °C,
  pause at 101 °C.
- **d3:** split pin. The Thor and Orin stay on 0.31.0; the Spark tests
  0.30.0; spark2 stays off 0.31.0 until strict + thinking is clean.
- **d4:** the Spark tests the eugr GB10 nightly.

## Findings to carry into R2 (t14–t17) and follow-ups

- **Mixed-engine pooling.** The mesh fingerprint has no engine version (c34).
  A 0.30.0 Spark and a nightly spark2 pool as one plain `cortex`, so measure
  through `cortex-spark` / `cortex-spark2`.
- **Memory gate.** On the Thor, a stopped engine's memory stays held until a
  page-cache clear runs after the stop. `lobes up` clears only before
  (documented in the runbook's Thor notes).
- **No page-cache grant.** The Orin and the Spark have none: sudo needs a
  password. The operator runs `orin` / `spark grant-permissions` themselves.
- **Orin chain mismatch.** The Orin's CLI chain includes audio files the
  gateway never had. Recreate there with the gateway's label chain and
  explicit `docker compose -f ... --no-deps`.
- **Governor (code follow-up).** It must refuse a container without explicit
  `/dev/nvidia*` devices, and its default bands don't fit a Spark whose acpitz
  zones idle around 88 °C.
- **Named costs:**
  - Spark cortex decode on 0.31.0 was about 83% of spark2's nightly (14.0 /
    14.4 vs 17.0 / 16.9 tok/s);
  - Orin embedder about 13% slower;
  - Orin reranker about 7% slower.
- **Filed issues:** #321 (`assess --port` drops the key) and #322 (`--probes
  --role cortex` ignores `--model`).
- **Lapses l1–l10** are filed, for adjudication at `/summarize-delivery`.
