## Why it matters

- the mesh trades cortex concurrency for execution speed on purpose: the Thor's dense 27B decodes at 12.1 tok/s, while a ~3B-active MoE on this same board measured 61.2 tok/s on an older engine — a fast doer is worth more here than a second cortex replica, and the operator has accepted the pool loss (q1). The aim is 100 tok/s single-stream on this board, which is ~1.6x the best figure ever measured here and is NOT expected from the reliable MTP lane alone — reaching it is what the fast gear (deeper MTP or DFlash) has to earn. The speed claim is the whole point, so it is measured single-stream on this box or it is not claimed
  - instruction: Every speed sentence in the docs must cite a transcript under docs/evidence/ taken on this box; vendor and forum figures are cited as external reference only, never as acceptance.
